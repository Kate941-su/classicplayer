No login is required. All features are free; there are no ads and no data collection.

About the audio content:
The app streams MP3 files published as free material by the CMSL Classical Masterpieces Sound Library (https://classical-sound.seesaa.net/, by ANDO TOWA). The site states that these files may be used freely, including commercially, under the Creative Commons Attribution 2.1 Japan license. The app does not re-host the files; it plays them directly from the CMSL servers and shows the credit in the Info tab and on each piece ("About this piece").

Composer portraits are public domain or freely licensed images from Wikimedia Commons, credited in Info > Image credits.

To test playback: tap "Shuffle everything" on the Home tab, or open a genre, a composer and a work, then tap "Play the whole work".
