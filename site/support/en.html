About the recordings

All audio comes from the free mp3 files published by CMSL クラシック名曲サウンドライブラリー (by ANDO TOWA) under Creative Commons Attribution 2.1 Japan. The app does not copy or redistribute the files; it streams them directly from CMSL's server.

This is not an official CMSL app. Please do not contact CMSL about this app.

FAQ

A track won't play

  • Playback needs an internet connection (offline downloads are not supported).
  • If CMSL removes or moves a recording, it can no longer be played. The catalog is updated monthly and unavailable tracks are removed; if one was in your Favorites, it is shown as "currently unavailable".
  • Playback stops after three tracks in a row fail. Please check your connection.

When is the catalog updated?

Automatically around the 1st of every month. You can also check for updates from the app's About screen.

Can I transfer my Favorites?

Favorites and history are stored only on your device, so they are not transferred when you change devices or reinstall (there are no accounts).

What does a tip unlock?

Nothing. Every track and feature is free. Tips are simply a way to support development; after a tip, a "Supporter" badge is shown. The badge is stored only on the device and disappears if you reinstall (there is nothing to restore).

Are there ads or data collection?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
